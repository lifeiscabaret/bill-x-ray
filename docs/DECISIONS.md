- **D-016** · 2026-11-23 · approved · News excerpts: this repository and the screen carry no verbatim excerpt from news articles of other outlets, Korean or foreign; they carry only the outlet, date, title, link and a fact sentence the team wrote itself. Seoul Economic Daily articles and legal originals (Korean National Assembly bills, statutes, the official gazette, court decisions; U.S. federal documents such as congress.gov and govinfo) may carry excerpts within the existing limits (300 characters for official documents, 200 for Seoul Economic Daily articles). A news excerpt needed for checking stays in the private research folder. The brand, data/API and full-article redistribution rights of the newsroom tracking project stay not cleared. This updates the README rights wording that D-004 kept.
- **D-017** · 2026-11-23 · approved · Cross-review verdicts use the review verdict format shared with ATLAS Lineage spec 04: one `review_verdict` record per judged row (id `<module>-RVW-<four digits>`, `row_id`, `verdict` exactly one of `load_as_is`, `load_after_fix` or `re_research`, `issues`, `action`, `packet`, `reviewer` as a tool or role label, `reviewed_at`). A row passes only if it has at least one verdict and every verdict is `load_as_is`. Verdict files stay in the private research folder.

A person signs up and deploys, after the internal sign-off; agents never sign up or deploy.
